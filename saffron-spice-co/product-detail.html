Royal Dum Mutton Biryani

Tender baby-lamb mutton marinated for 24 hours in yoghurt, fried onion and hand-ground masala, layered with aged 1121 basmati, kewra water and Kashmiri saffron — sealed with dough and cooked slowly on dum.

🚚 Free delivery over Rs. 5,000  •  Ready in 60–75 minutes

A celebration-grade biryani built on patience. The mutton is first marinated overnight with raw papaya, yoghurt, ginger-garlic and our house biryani masala. Basmati is parboiled to 70%, layered with the meat, saffron milk, fried onion, mint and ghee, then sealed and cooked over a low flame for 55 minutes.

The result: every grain separate, perfumed and distinct — with meat that falls from the bone.

Cuisine Mughlai / Punjabi
Cooking method Dum (sealed-pot slow steam), 55 minutes
Rice Aged 1121 Sella basmati (2 years)
Protein Baby-lamb mutton, bone-in — 450 g (full portion)
Serves 2–3 adults
Net weight 1.1 kg (rice + meat)
Energy ~ 780 kcal per serving
Spice level Medium (adjustable)
Shelf life Best consumed within 4 hours; refrigerate up to 24 hours
Packaging Clay-effect, recyclable dum pot with sealed lid

Ingredients: mutton, basmati rice, yoghurt, onion, desi ghee, ginger, garlic, tomato, green chilli, mint, coriander, kewra water, saffron, biryani masala (coriander, cumin, black cardamom, mace, nutmeg, cinnamon, black pepper, bay leaf), salt.

Allergens: contains milk (yoghurt, ghee). Prepared in a kitchen that also handles nuts and gluten.

Delivery: insulated royal box, hot on arrival. Standard delivery 45–75 minutes within our city zones.

Reheating: sprinkle 2 tbsp water, cover tightly and heat on low flame for 8 minutes, or microwave covered for 2–3 minutes.
