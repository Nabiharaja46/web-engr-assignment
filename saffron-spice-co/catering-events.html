Event FAQs

How far ahead should we book?

We recommend 3–4 weeks for weddings and 7 days for corporate events, though we do accommodate shorter notice where we can.

Do you provide service staff and equipment?

Yes — uniformed waiters, chafing dishes, crockery and live-station chefs are available as add-ons.

Can you cater to dietary requirements?

Absolutely. Vegetarian, low-oil and nut-free menus can be prepared with advance notice.

Is a tasting available?

A complimentary tasting for up to four people is included with bookings above Rs. 250,000.
