Mutton Dum Biryani at Home

Chef Rashid’s step-by-step guide to a restaurant-grade pot.

8 hrsMarinate
1 hrCook
5Serves
MediumDifficulty

Biryani is patience dressed up as a meal. This is the recipe our Lahore kitchen taught three generations of cooks — scaled down for a home stove.

Ingredients

  • Mutton, bone-in1 kg
  • Basmati rice, soaked500 g
  • Yoghurt, whisked250 g
  • Fried onions (birista)200 g
  • Ginger-garlic paste2 tbsp
  • Biryani masala3 tbsp
  • Saffron in warm milkpinch
  • Kewra water1 tbsp
  • Desi ghee100 g

Method

  1. Marinate overnight

    Mix mutton with yoghurt, ginger-garlic, half the birista, masala, salt and a little oil. Cover and refrigerate for at least 8 hours.

  2. Parboil the rice

    Boil rice in salted water with whole spices until 70% cooked — the grain should snap, not crumble. Drain.

  3. Layer the pot

    Spread meat across the base. Cover with rice, then scatter birista, mint, coriander, saffron milk, kewra and ghee.

  4. Seal & dum

    Cover with foil and a tight lid or dough ring. Cook on high for 3 minutes, then very low for 45 minutes.

  5. Rest & serve

    Turn off the heat and rest for 15 minutes without opening. Fold gently from the bottom and serve with raita.

Chef’s tipUse a heavy-bottomed pot and place a hot tawa under it — it prevents the bottom layer from catching.
